## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the intersection of biomedical engineering precision and haute joaillerie dental artistry. Designed for elite prosthodontists, cosmetic dentists, master ceramists, and high-volume CAD/CAM production teams, the interface projects absolute authority, surgical exactness, and artisanal mastery.

The visual direction combines dark-mode luxury minimalism with technical instrumentation:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep obsidian and charcoal matrix reminiscent of high-end optical scanning environments and luxury editorial lookbooks.
- **Materiality:** Satin metallic gold accents, micron-precise hair-thin borders, and low-diffuse ambient illumination that simulates studio lighting on ceramic restorations.
- **Emotional Impact:** Reassuring permanence, ultra-precise CAD/CAM fidelity, and exquisite craftsmanship for high-end anterior aesthetics, implant superstructures, and monolithic zirconia fabrication.

## Colors

The color palette translates the craft of dental ceramic tinting, zirconia sintering, and CAD/CAM optics into a disciplined digital spectrum:

- **Primary (`#D4AF37` — Polished Dental Gold):** Applied to primary actions, selected case status checkpoints, restoration margin confirmations, and high-tier verification badges.
- **Secondary (`#F3E5AB` — Warm Champagne):** Used for micro-copy callouts, shade-guide highlights (e.g., VITA Classical Bleach shades), active toggle states, and secondary telemetry readouts.
- **Tertiary (`#9E7B35` — Antique Bronze / Sintered Gold):** Governs structural hair-thin borders, timeline connectors, inactive tab states, and calibrated depth indicators.
- **Neutral Core (`#121316` — Deep Onyx):** The structural foundation. Layered through graded charcoal surfaces:
  - Base Canvas: `#0A0B0D` (True Onyx Void)
  - Card / Surface Tier 1: `#121316` (Deep Charcoal Studio)
  - Hover / Elevation Tier 2: `#1A1C21` (Refined Graphite)
  - Surface Border: `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)` (Micron Gold Filament)
- **Functional State Accents:**
  - Clinical Success / Pass: `#78B159`
  - Marginal Discrepancy / Alert: `#D97736`
  - Critical CAD Occlusal Collision / Error: `#C24141`
  - Optical Scan Blue / 3D Sensor: `#6495ED`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes classical restorative craftsmanship with modern micro-milling precision:

- **Display & Headings (`EB Garamond`):** Evokes anatomical mastery, dental aesthetic literature, and bespoke cosmetic artistry. Always set in natural sentence case or title case; never forced uppercase. Italics are reserved strictly for clinical substantiation, material classifications (e.g., *Multi-layer Gradient Zirconia*), or case notes.
- **Body (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Delivers clean optical legibility across complex work orders, lab prescription slips, patient anatomical records, and multi-step milling queues.
- **Labels & Technical Instrumentation (`Geist`):** Monospaced numeric behavior and monoline purity make it optimal for micrometric tolerances, VITA shade matching codes (e.g., `OM1`, `A2`, `3M2`), 5-axis toolpath coordinates, and STL/PLY file metadata. Always rendered with heightened letter-spacing for razor-sharp clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model reflects the structured discipline of CAD/CAM toolbars and cleanroom clinical workspaces:

- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12-column dynamic grid with `margin: 3rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. CAD viewers, 3D mesh inspection panels, and tooth arch maps lock to rigid 4px rhythmic multiples. Split-view configurations anchor technical parameters to a fixed 380px inspector panel on the right, allowing the 3D viewport or order stream to remain fluid.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Side panels fold into overlay sheets with high-contrast hair-line gold triggers.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 0.75rem`. Complex dental charts collapse into sequential card flows with tooth-by-tooth swipe cards.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Generous external margins give the brand breathing room, contrasting tightly spaced, data-dense internal metrics where sub-millimeter tolerances are detailed.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface containment, micron-grade metallic borders, and restrained warm-tinted luminescence rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Floor/Viewport Canvas):** `#0A0B0D`. Recessed, non-reflective backdrop for 3D model orientation and panoramic X-ray evaluation.
- **Level 1 (Clinical Cards & Work Order Surfaces):** `#121316` outlined with `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.14)`. No shadow; depth is marked purely by edge contrast.
- **Level 2 (Active Inspections, Flyouts, Toolbars):** `#1A1C21` with `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)` and a subtle ambient gold glow: `0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 16px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Shade Calibration Overlays):** `#1E2026` with `1px solid #D4AF37` hair-lines and heavy backdrop occlusion: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px) brightness(0.7)`.

## Shapes

The interface adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** geometry (`0.25rem` / `4px` base radius). 

Dental CAD software, precision titanium abutment milling, and diamond-bur micro-carving mandate exactness; rounded, playful bubbly corners are strictly avoided. Buttons, inputs, tab indicators, and data chips use precise 4px corners, evoking milled zirconia blocks, glass ceramic ingots, and surgical cassette hardware. Only circular tooth position indicators (Universal or FDI notation) retain full `rounded-full` curvature to match anatomical numbering standards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Master Lab Action / Submit Case):** Solid polished metallic gold background (`#D4AF37`), dark graphite text (`#0A0B0D`), font weight 600, uppercase letter-spaced `Geist` label, 4px border radius. Hover: `#F3E5AB` with subtle gold aura. Active: `#9E7B35`.
- **Secondary (Inspect STL / Adjust Margin):** Matte charcoal surface (`#16181D`), 1px perimeter border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`), warm champagne typography (`#F3E5AB`).
- **Ghost / Tertiary (Export Diagnostics):** Transparent background, pale gold text (`rgba(243, 229, 171, 0.8)`), no outline until hover, which triggers `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)` background fill.

### Input Fields & Select Menus
- **State Styling:** Deep charcoal input tray (`#0E1013`), 1px muted gold border (`rgba(158, 123, 53, 0.35)`), crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), micro-label in uppercase gold above the input.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#D4AF37` with `0 0 8px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)` edge illumination. Never use generic browser focus rings.

### Cards & Work Order Tiles
- Encased in `#121316` with a razor-thin gold header divider (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`).
- Top right quadrant dedicated to case metadata (e.g., `CASE #9482`, `MAT: KATANA™ HTML`) rendered in `label-sm` monospaced font.
- Dynamic status stripe on card edge indicating lab stage: Intake, CAD Design, Sintering, Hand Characterization, Final Glaze, QC Passed.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact rectangular forms (radius: 4px).
- **Shade Chips (e.g., A1, B1, BL2):** Split visual layout showing true digital color swatch alongside technical alphanumeric code.
- **Status Chips:** Matte black surface with a 1px colored micro-ring and 6px illuminated status jewel (Emerald for Approved, Gold for Ceramist In-Progress, Ruby for Remake/Adjustment Required).

### Tooth Selector & Odontogram
- High-contrast anatomical arch mapping interface.
- Unselected teeth delineated by muted graphite contours (`#2A2D35`). Selected restorations illuminate with a rich brushed gold gradient and active restoration category tags (e.g., `#11 - Veneer`, `#14 - Screw-Retained Crown`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 16px frames with 2px corner radius. Unchecked: `#121316` frame with `#333742` border. Checked: `#D4AF37` fill with dark onyx checkmark glyph.
